How to play

Five to ten of you meet in a back hall and swear the oath together. A few of you have already sold the hall to the Qing court. Those informers know each other; the brothers know nothing. Over five missions the brotherhood tries to carry three through, and the informers try to wreck three. Talk as much as you like, about anything, at any time.

Setup

Everyone gets a secret card. The informers then get a moment to recognise each other (on this site, your card lists their names). One player is picked at random to lead first.

A round

  1. The leader picks who goes. Exactly as many people as the mission needs (table below), and the leader may pick themself.
  2. Everyone votes on that team, all at once, then the votes are shown. A majority sends them out. A tie sends nobody. If the team is rejected, the next player to the leader's left leads and picks again.
  3. The team goes out. Each member secretly plays a Success or a Fail card. Brothers must play Success; informers may play either. The cards are shuffled and revealed, so nobody knows who played what. One Fail wrecks the mission.

Five rejected teams in one round and the Qing win on the spot. A rejection is never free.

Winning

The game ends the moment either side reaches three missions: three carried through and the brotherhood wins; three wrecked and the Qing win. The Qing also win if five teams are rejected in a single round.

Advice

  • Brothers: get yourself sent out. Watch who approves what, and who was out on which mission. A team that came back clean is worth sending again.
  • Informers: behave like a brother. Vote the way a brother would. Wrecking the very first mission is the fastest way to be found out.
  • Everyone: a rejected team costs one of five. Use them, but not all of them.

Options on this site

Blind informers skips the recognition: the informers do not know each other either.

The Hour deck adds eight face-up cards. One is drawn at the start of every round and holds for that round only. Drawn cards are not returned, so a five-round game shows five of the eight, and anyone can count what is left.

  • Travel Light: the team goes one person short. Never drawn for a two-person team.
  • Signed: the cards are not shuffled, so everyone sees who played what.
  • Orders from Above: informers on the team must play Fail. A success therefore clears the whole team.
  • Laid Up: one player, picked at random, cannot be sent this round. Never drawn for the fifth mission.
  • Hold Your Tongue: nobody talks this round.
  • Quiet Night: nothing happens.
  • Lights Out: only the tally of each vote is shown. Who voted which way stays hidden until the game ends.
  • Recused: the leader may not put themself on the team. Never drawn for the last two missions, and otherwise only when the other seats still hold enough brothers for a clean team.

玩法說明

五到十個人在香堂裡歃血為盟。其中幾個人早就把這座堂口賣給了清廷。這些密探彼此認識，其他兄弟什麼都不知道。五次任務裡，天地會要辦成三次，密探要搞砸三次。你可以隨時說任何話。

準備

每個人拿到一張祕密身分牌。接著密探互相認一下（在這個網站上，你的牌會直接列出同夥的名字）。隨機選一個人先當隊長。

一個回合

  1. 隊長挑人出去辦事。人數要剛好等於這次任務需要的人數（見下表），隊長可以把自己放進去。
  2. 所有人投票，同時亮票。過半數贊成就出發；平手算否決。被否決的話，隊長換給左手邊的人，重新挑人。
  3. 隊伍出去辦事。每個隊員祕密出一張「成功」或「失敗」。兄弟只能出成功，密探兩種都可以。牌洗過後翻開，所以沒人知道是誰出的。只要有一張失敗，這件事就砸了。

同一回合連續五次提名都被否決，清廷直接獲勝。否決不是免費的。

勝負

任一方累積三次任務，遊戲立刻結束：三次辦成，天地會贏；三次搞砸，清廷贏。同一回合五次提名被否決，也算清廷贏。

建議

  • 兄弟：想辦法讓自己被派出去。記住誰贊成了什麼、誰上過哪次任務。乾淨回來的隊伍值得再派一次。
  • 密探：表現得像個兄弟。投票要投得像兄弟。第一次任務就搞砸，是最快被抓到的方法。
  • 所有人：一次否決就用掉五次裡的一次。可以用，但別全用光。

網站上的選項

盲密探：跳過認人的步驟，密探也不知道彼此是誰。

天時牌：加入八張明牌。每回合開始翻一張，只在那一回合有效。翻過的不放回，所以五回合的一局會看到其中五張，剩下哪幾張誰都算得出來。

  • 輕裝：這一趟少帶一個人。隊伍只有兩人時不會抽到。
  • 畫押：牌不洗，誰出什麼全桌看得見。
  • 密令：隊上的密探必須出失敗。所以這趟若成功，全隊清白。
  • 掛彩：隨機一人這一趟派不出去。第五次任務不會抽到。
  • 封口：這一回合不許說話。
  • 無事：什麼都沒發生。
  • 熄燈：每次投票只公布票數，誰投了什麼要到遊戲結束才揭曉。
  • 避嫌：隊長不能派自己。最後兩次任務不會抽到，其他任務也只在隊長以外的人還湊得出一支全是兄弟的隊伍時才會抽到。
